## 1. Primary Resources

Wikipedia organizes encyclopedia articles about people, places, organizations, events, and other subjects. I examined the Jordan national football team article because soccer is one of my interests. The article is a digital resource describing a real-world team. Its text, photographs, tables, and references provide different ways to understand that subject.

The page includes sections about history, players, results, and competitive records. Its information box summarizes important details, while references identify supporting sources. These elements organize information within the article. Related articles about players, competitions, and football organizations are separate resources connected to it.

## 2. Supported Interactions

Wikipedia supports searching, browsing, reading, and checking sources. A reader can enter a topic in the search box or follow a link from another article. Within the Jordan article, the table of contents lets readers jump directly to sections such as Players or Competitive record.

Readers can also follow numbered citations to investigate supporting evidence. However, the history section I viewed displayed a warning requesting more citations. This shows that access to references does not guarantee that every statement is equally supported.

These interactions serve different needs: searching locates a known topic, browsing discovers related information, and checking references helps evaluate claims.

## 3. Classification System and Effectiveness

Wikipedia uses a category system with broader and narrower groupings. The Jordan article belongs to categories including Asian national association football teams and National sports teams established in 1953. One groups teams geographically, while the other groups them by establishment year.

The Asian category contains subcategories and an alphabetical list of articles. This demonstrates hierarchical organization. However, articles can belong to several categories, so the overall structure is an overlapping network rather than one simple tree. These multiple perspectives resemble facets, but they are not presented as combined filtering controls.

For students and soccer fans, this structure supports discovery without knowing every article title. However, category links are near the bottom of the article and may be overlooked. The Asian category also contains several different subgroup types, which can make browsing less predictable. Clearer explanations of these groupings could help beginners.

## 4. Relationships Between Resources

Wikipedia represents relationships through links in article text, category membership, and navigation boxes. In the Jordan article, the Jordan Football Association link connects the team to its governing organization. Competition links connect it to the wider tournament context.

Categories connect the article to similar resources. For example, the Asian teams category lists Jordan alongside other national teams. Navigation boxes provide additional routes through related subjects, including Football in Jordan and national teams of Asia.

These methods serve different purposes: contextual links explain specific relationships, categories group shared characteristics, and navigation boxes offer convenient browsing routes. Together, they allow readers to explore beyond one article.
